# 02 · Divisibility Report

**Topic:** AND, inclusive OR and XOR

Ask for a whole number and report whether it is divisible by both 4 and 5, by at least one of 4 or 5, and by exactly one of 4 or 5. Calculate `divisibleBy4` and `divisibleBy5`, then use `&&`, `||`, and `^` for the three results.

## Requirements

- Use all three operators `&&`, `||`, and `^`.

Values entered after prompts are typed by the user.

```console
Enter an integer:
10
Divisible by 4 and 5: false
Divisible by 4 or 5: true
Divisible by 4 or 5 but not both: true
```

```console
Enter an integer:
20
Divisible by 4 and 5: true
Divisible by 4 or 5: true
Divisible by 4 or 5 but not both: false
```
